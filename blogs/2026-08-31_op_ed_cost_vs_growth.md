A company (A) recently adopted AI tooling into their SDLC, and subsequently fired a number employees, including software developers and others. 
It was a carefully deliberated, rational choice, not a rushed decision based on hype and bluster.

- They had trained staff in using the AI tools and noticed enhanced productivity.
- They had evaluated the AI output and found it, for their needs, an adequate replacement of human efforts.
- They had carefully considered the running costs of their AI systems and found it much lower than staffing costs.
