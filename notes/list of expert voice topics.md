# about targeting the content (tof, mof, bof) 
With content creation, you are trying to achieve three things:
- attract an audience.
- build credibility.
- drive conversion.

These are very different outcomes, each requiring different types of content. To achieve all three goals, the content must balance these three targets.

To target the Top of Funnel (TOF), publish broad, relatable, and highly shareable content. This helps you build a growing audience and get discovered. Compose for the broadest audience. Focus on industry trends, getting-started guides, and resource lists. Define your unique voice and use it. Don't be afraid to be quirky. Your goal is growing reach and attention.

To target the Middle of Funnel (MOF), you publish more niche, specific content like how-to guides, option A vs option B comparisons, deep dives, process breakdowns. This helps your audience build their trust in you. Your goal is to demonstrate expertise and make your thinking visible. It might be a good idea to tone down the quirkiness, while keeping recognisable elements of your voice.

By now you should have the attention of promising prospects, but they still need proof. To target the Bottom of Funnel (BOF) you need to demonstrate experience, not just knowledge. Produce case studies, testimonials, client results, before-and-after stories, service breakdowns, product demos, tutorials, etc. The goal is to create demand, reduce uncertainty, and nudge decision makers closer to action.

If your content is only TOF, you might build up an audience, but aren't building trust. If only BOF, you could earn credibility, but a lack of spread will leave your impact small. If only MOF, you might achieve neither.
